# ART.md

Every image the v2 site needs, with its prompt. Direction lives in `DESIGN.md`.

## How to generate

1. Use **one Gemini conversation** for all the art, so the style and the samurai stay
   the same. Start it with the style lock below, then send the prompts in order.
2. Every scene is **built from components**, one image per element, and assembled in code.
   Each component moves at its own depth, which is what makes the parallax read.
3. Only things with a drawn shape are images (moon, clouds, ridges, figures, plants).
   Light is made in code: the sky gradient, the moon's glow, mist, light shafts, embers.
4. Two kinds of background:
   - **green**: flat `#00FF00`, keyed out. For solid things that must hide what is behind
     them (ridges, clouds, figures, plants)
   - **black**: pure `#000000`, blended with `screen`, so black disappears and only the
     light remains. For things that glow (the moon)
5. Light comes from the moon in the upper left, so every component has its red rim on its
   **top and left** edges. Say so in every prompt (it is already in the prompts below).

## Asset standard

Every asset follows the same rules, from the generated source to the file the page loads.

**Names**: `<scene>-<component>[-<n>]`, English, lowercase, words joined by hyphens.
`<scene>` is one of `hero`, `about`, `arsenal`, `work`, `contact`. `<n>` numbers repeats
of one component (`hero-cloud-1`, `hero-cloud-2`). The names in this file are the names
to use.

**Sources** (`assets-src/`, committed): the chosen file only, as downloaded, full size.

```
assets-src/
  <scene>/
    <scene>-<component>.png|jpg|mp4   the one in use
    _tries/                           other attempts, optional, never loaded
```

**Web files** (`public/art/`, generated by `scripts/build-art.sh`, never edited by hand):

```
public/art/
  <scene>/
    <component>-<width>.webp          still image, two widths (small for phones, large)
    <component>/<nnn>.webp            image sequence (frames 000, 001, ...)
```

Green sources come out with transparency, cropped to the drawn shape. A new or changed
source means running the script again, not editing the output.

## Style lock (send first)

```
For every image in this conversation, use one consistent style:
anime key-frame illustration, clean ink linework, flat cel shading, very high contrast.
Night. Almost everything is in deep shadow: 80 to 90 percent of every frame is near black
(#050405 to #1a1012). The ONLY light source in this world is red: a blood-red moon,
red rim light on edges, embers, red lanterns. No white light, no blue, no daylight,
no fill light, no colour other than black and red shades. Forms are read from red rim
light and silhouette. Painterly mist in dark red-black. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
Reply only with images.
```

## 1. Hero: the samurai under the red moon

Assembled back to front. Made in code, no image: the sky gradient, the moon's glow,
the mist between the ridges, the light shafts, embers.

| Depth | Component | Moves |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (far) | moon | barely |
| 2 | clouds, two strips | slow, and drift sideways |
| 3 | far ridge | slow |
| 4 | middle ridge | medium |
| 5 | near ridge | fast |
| 6 | samurai | with the scroll scrub |
| 7 (near) | foreground branch | fastest, sweeps out to reveal About |

Generate in the order the prompts appear below: moon, the three ridges (far to near,
so their darkness steps down evenly), the clouds (matched to the middle ridge), the
branch, then the samurai.

no clouds, no stars. Background: pure flat black #000000.

**`hero-samurai-key`** (9:16, green)
```
Vertical 9:16 character key frame of a mysterious, menacing samurai, seen almost only
as a silhouette.

FRAMING: waist-up only. The top of his hat is about 10 percent below the top edge; the
image is cut just below his waist sash at the bottom edge. No legs, no ground.
He occupies the right two thirds of the frame; the left third is empty green (room for
the sword swing).

DIRECTION: he faces the LEFT side of the frame. Three-quarter back view: we see his
right shoulder blade and back more than his chest. His head is turned further left
and slightly down, and from under the hat brim he looks back toward the viewer.

COSTUME: a wide conical straw kasa hat pulled low, its rim slightly frayed. A plain
black kimono with wide sleeves, a dark obi sash tied at the waist, black hakama
starting below the sash. A long red scarf wrapped around his neck, its two frayed
ends streaming behind him to the right in the wind. Dark cloth wraps on his forearms.
A katana in a black scabbard pushed through the obi at his left hip, hilt pointing
forward and up toward the left.

POSE: ready to draw. Shoulders raised and tense, body leaning slightly forward.
Left hand grips the scabbard near the guard, thumb pushing the guard forward to free
the blade. Right hand rests on the hilt, fingers half closed, elbow raised.

FACE: completely lost in the black shadow of the hat: no nose, no mouth, no features.
Only ONE small sharp glint of red light where his left eye is, looking at the viewer.

LIGHT: the whole figure, skin, hands, cloth, is flat pure black (#050405) with no
interior shading and no red tint. The only light is a thin, sharp blood-red rim line
from behind-left along his outer edges: hat brim, shoulder, sleeve, hilt. The scarf
is the only part with red colour.

STYLE: anime key frame, clean ink linework, flat cel shading, very high contrast.

BACKGROUND: solid flat bright chroma green #00FF00, perfectly even, no gradient, no
shadow on it, no green reflected on the figure. Nothing else in the image.
```

**`hero-samurai`** (Veo, image-to-video from `hero-samurai-key`)
```
CAMERA: locked-off static camera. No zoom, no pan, no tilt, no camera shake. The
first frame is identical to the start image, and the framing stays exactly the same
for the whole clip.

SWORD: a full-length katana. When drawn, the blade is as long as its scabbard, clearly
longer than his whole arm, with a gentle curve. Plain dark steel with a thin red edge
highlight.

ACTION, in this order (an iaijutsu draw, deliberate and heavy, slightly slowed
like a dramatic anime cut, so every stage is clearly visible):
1. 0.0 to 1.0 s: he stands still, only the scarf moving in the wind. A slow breath
   lifts his shoulders.
2. 1.0 to 2.0 s: his left thumb pushes the guard; the blade slides out of the
   scabbard slowly, steel showing inch by inch.
3. 2.0 to 3.0 s: his right hand draws the katana fully out and forward to the LEFT,
   the long blade sweeping horizontally across the left side of the frame.
4. 3.0 to 4.0 s: the blade continues into a rising diagonal cut toward the upper
   left, his body twisting into it, the scarf whipping hard.
5. 4.0 s to end: he freezes in the finished pose, blade held high to the upper left.
   The scarf settles slowly. Nothing else moves.

NO EFFECTS: no slash trail, no motion arc, no sparkle, no lens flare, no glow. Only the
figure and the sword.

The whole blade stays inside the frame at every moment.

LOOK: he stays a silhouette the whole time: face hidden in the hat's shadow, body flat
black, only thin blood-red rim lines on his edges. No new light on him, no face
revealed, no change to his costume or design. Anime style, crisp frames, no motion
blur smearing the figure.

BACKGROUND: solid flat bright #00FF00 throughout, unchanged. No other objects, no text.
```
Check before keeping: the camera never moves, the blade stays inside the frame, the green
stays flat from start to end.

## 2. About: the falls

left. Made in code, no image: the night sky, the glow on the water, the pool, the spray
and mist, and the water's flow (the falls image is scrolled down inside itself).

| Depth | Component | Moves |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (far) | about-cliff: the cliff with the gap the water falls through | slow |
| 2 | about-falls: the falling water alone | flows down on its own |
| 3 | about-rocks: flat rocks around the pool | medium |
| 4 | about-figure: the samurai kneeling on a rock | medium, with the rocks |
| 5 (near) | about-front: reeds along the bottom | fastest, sweeps in from the hero |
